UASReady is a tactical and commercial preflight safety decision-support engine engineered specifically for Part 107 commercial remote pilots, public safety agencies, first responders, and critical infrastructure drone operators.

Engineered for handheld drone ground stations and controllers—including the DJI RC Pro Enterprise—UASReady provides immediate, 100% deterministic flight-readiness verdicts (GO / CAUTION / NO-GO) backed by explainable aviation rules and transparent thresholds.

--------------------------------------------------
⚡ MISSION-CRITICAL FEATURES
--------------------------------------------------

🛰️ 100% DETERMINISTIC SAFETY ENGINE
Zero opaque AI hallucinations or probabilistic guessing. Every preflight verdict is evaluated against deterministic aviation parameters, Part 107 regulations, and manufacturer technical limits with clear rationales and parameter audits.

🗺️ LIVE FAA CONTROLLED & SPECIAL USE AIRSPACE
• Live 30 NM radius query directly from public FAA open aeronautical data feeds.
• Class B, Class C, Class D, and surface-based Class E airport extensions.
• Special Use Airspace (SUA) overlays: Prohibited, Restricted, Warning, Alert, and Military Operations Areas (MOA).
• Filtered to display operational surface-to-500' airspace to eliminate high-altitude clutter.
• Interactive tap-to-inspect airspace boundaries, ceiling, floor (MSL/AGL), and active restrictions.

📻 AIRPORT OVERLAY & CTAF/TOWER FREQUENCIES
Instantly resolves manned airfields and active runway geometries within 30 NM. Displays active CTAF, Tower, or UNICOM frequencies and distance directly on your launch dashboard to maintain critical situational awareness.

⛅ LIVE WEATHER & SOLAR EPHEMERIS
• Real-time sustained wind, wind gusts, temperature, precipitation, and cloud base ceiling.
• Local civil dawn, sunrise, sunset, and civil dusk ephemeris calculations.
• Part 107 daylight minutes remaining and night flight anti-collision lighting indicators.

🌌 NOAA SPACE WEATHER & GNSS RELIABILITY
Monitors live planetary Kp-index and geomagnetic storm telemetry from NOAA's Space Weather Prediction Center (SWPC) to alert against GPS scintillation, multi-path degradation, and compass heading interference before takeoff.

🚁 ENTERPRISE FLEET SPECIFICATIONS

📋 AVIATION CHECKLISTS & SOP PROCEDURES
• Integrated preflight, launch readiness, and postflight secure checklists.
• Interactive custom checklist item manager for agency-specific SOP additions.
• 10-Step Emergency Protocol covering RTH failure, emergency descent, critical battery management, signal lost-link, obstacle hazards, and regulatory evasive action reporting.
